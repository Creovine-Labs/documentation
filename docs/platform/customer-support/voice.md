# Localized voice

Lira can talk to your customers, not just type. The same knowledge-grounded
agent that powers chat can answer out loud in a **natural, localized voice** —
starting with **Nigerian** voices — so a caller hears a warm, on-brand agent
that actually understands them instead of a robotic, foreign-sounding IVR.

It is the same brain as chat: answers are grounded in your knowledge base, the
agent can take approved actions, and anything it cannot resolve is escalated to
your team with full context.

:::info Plan requirement
Localized voice is available on the **Scale** and **Enterprise** plans. It is
enabled for your organization during onboarding. Voice usage is metered per
minute on top of your plan.
:::

## How it works

The voice experience is turn-based and hands-free — the customer just talks:

1. The customer speaks; their speech is transcribed.
2. Lira answers from your knowledge base and, where relevant, takes an action.
3. The reply is spoken back in your chosen localized voice.
4. For account actions (for example freezing a card), Lira shows an on-screen
   confirmation the customer taps to approve — the action runs only after
   approval, with a full audit trail.

Customers can speak their choice or tap on-screen option chips, and can type at
any time instead of talking.

## Choosing a voice

- **Scale** — choose from Lira's curated library of localized voices (Nigerian
  voices available today, with more accents added over time). Lira manages the
  voice provider, quality, and licensing for you.
- **Enterprise** — optionally commission a **custom brand voice**: your own
  chosen voice, cloned with the speaker's written consent, so the agent sounds
  unmistakably like your brand. This is set up with our team as part of your
  deployment.

We do not offer self-serve voice uploads: cloning a real person's voice
requires their consent, and we manage voice quality and cost on your behalf so
every call sounds right.

## What the voice agent can do

Everything the chat agent can, spoken:

- Answer questions grounded in your knowledge base.
- Look things up and take approved actions in your systems (with the same
  approval policies and audit trail as chat).
- Offer choices as on-screen chips the customer can tap or say.
- Escalate to a human with full conversation context when needed.

## Getting started

Localized voice is enabled per organization during onboarding. If you are on
Scale or Enterprise and want it turned on — or you are on Pro and want to
upgrade for voice — [talk to our team](https://liraintelligence.com/contact).
