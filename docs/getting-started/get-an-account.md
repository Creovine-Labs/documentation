# Get an Account

Lira is **self-serve**. Go to [liraintelligence.com/signup](https://liraintelligence.com/signup), create an account, and stand up your own organization — no sales call, no card, no invite code.

Enterprise is the one exception: custom brand voice, SSO/SAML, custom retention and volume pricing are arranged with our team.

## How signup works

### 1. Create your account

Go to **[liraintelligence.com/signup](https://liraintelligence.com/signup)** and sign up with your email and a password. Verify your email and you're in.

If someone sent you a concierge invite link (`?invite=...`), it still works — it pre-fills the form and attaches your new account to the organization that invited you. It is no longer required.

### 2. Create your organization

On first login, the onboarding flow creates your **organization**. This is the tenant that owns your knowledge, your conversations, your tickets, and your billing. You become its owner.

You'll get:

- Your Lira **org ID** and a unique widget secret (used for signed identity)
- An **invite code** (`LRA-XXXX`) so teammates can join
- The **Free** plan by default, with its usage limits — see [Subscription & Billing](/getting-started/plans-and-billing)

New organizations start in the [sandbox](/platform/customer-support/sandbox-and-going-live). Paid billing only begins when you upgrade and complete the Paddle-backed subscription flow.

### 3. Activate support

In the dashboard, **Support → Activate** walks through:

1. **Email setup** — choose a Lira-hosted support address (e.g. `support@your-company.lira.email`) or forward from your own domain.
2. **Knowledge base** — upload docs, paste a website URL to crawl, or connect Google Drive as a source. See [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base/overview).
3. **Channel** — pick the chat widget, the full support page, or both. See [SDKs overview](/platform/customer-support/sdks).
4. **Test & go live** — chat with your own Lira in sandbox, confirm it sounds right, then flip the toggle to live.

Lira's own support agent is available in the dashboard throughout — it is grounded in these docs and can answer setup questions as you go.

### 4. Add your team

From **Members**, invite teammates by email. Each gets a one-time, expiring invite link tied to their address and scoped to a role (admin or member). Seats are unlimited on every plan, including Free.

Teammates already on Lira under another organization are attached to yours — they'll see both in their org switcher.

### 5. Install

Paste the widget snippet into your site or app. See the [install guides](/platform/customer-support/integration-guides) for Next.js, Vite, Remix, Rails, Django, Express, or plain HTML.

## Need a second organization?

Create it from the org switcher. Each organization has its own plan, limits, knowledge, and billing.

## Want to talk to a human first?

You don't have to, but you can. Email **team@liraintelligence.com**, or use **Speak to an expert** on the site — required only for Enterprise.
